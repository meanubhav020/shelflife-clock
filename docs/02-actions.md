FICTIONAL POLICY, FOR TRAINING USE ONLY

# The five actions

[REF-ACT-01] MONITOR. Re-check in 7 days. Use when the batch is outside scope (REF-SCORE-01), or its unsold share is below 0.10, or no other action is eligible under REF-ACT-02 to REF-ACT-05. State which reason applies.

[REF-ACT-02] TRANSFER. Move stock to another location that already stocks the same SKU and has a higher units_sold_last_30d for it. Allowed only if 21 or more days remain (transit takes up to 3 days). The target must also satisfy REF-CC-01 and REF-HC-02.

[REF-ACT-03] MARKDOWN. Food only. With 15 to 30 days remaining: up to 30 percent off. With 14 days or fewer remaining and not yet expired: up to 50 percent off. Never for HEALTHCARE (REF-HC-01). Not covered when more than 30 days remain.

[REF-ACT-04] RETURN TO SUPPLIER. Allowed only if supplier_accepts_returns is Y and 30 or more days remain. Credit terms are in REF-SUP-01.

[REF-ACT-05] DISPOSE. Donation: food only, with 3 to 14 days remaining. Destruction and write-off: all expired stock. Disposal vendors and their fees are not covered by this pack.

[REF-ACT-06] Order of preference when more than one action is eligible: TRANSFER, then RETURN TO SUPPLIER, then MARKDOWN, then DISPOSE.
