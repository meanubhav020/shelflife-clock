FICTIONAL POLICY, FOR TRAINING USE ONLY

# Healthcare products

[REF-HC-01] Healthcare products are never marked down and never donated.

[REF-HC-02] Healthcare batches may be transferred only to locations with healthcare_licensed = Y, and only with 21 or more days remaining.

[REF-HC-03] Healthcare stock past its expiry date must be quarantined at once and destroyed through a licensed disposal vendor (DISPOSE).

[REF-HC-04] A healthcare batch with 20 days or fewer remaining that cannot be transferred or returned is escalated to the QA Lead for a decision. The agent must not propose a disposition.
